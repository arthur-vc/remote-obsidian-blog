### Magic System
Magic exists in this world, but it marks you. Discrimination against magic users - stems from an ancient war long ago. 
Signs that someones used magic:
- Most common is the eyes, the pupils go from black to gold gradually, depending on how much you use it
- Scars - super extensive use of magic strains your body, leading to lightning like scars all over your body
- Another common sign - veins become a different colour, very gradual, need daily use to see noticeable effect
Scientists have learned to capture trace amounts of magical essence from the world, spots where magic leaks through the line between worlds have more magical essence to work with. 
Laboratories in the mountain??
Very mundane gadgets have been realised. 
Theory that it is simply the physics of another world leaking into ours and decaying/allowing us to do things deemed impossible.
Magic users are rare now, and most are heavily sought after for armys or private bodyguards for the elites. 

### City - Virilion - the City of Mist
Built atop the remains of an old ancient city (catacombs beneath, extensive. Some caves lead to massive caverns with whole city sectors that have fallen through.) Only vestiges of the ancient city remain. The rest has been rebuilt with darkend stone and wood (bloodbourne aesthetic), in concentric rings. The city sits on a penisula - more like an island connected by a thin causeway - and sits perched on this large hill. The whole city is structured in concentric rings leading up this steep hill, and on the pinnical is an ornate victorian church, looming high above all else, closest to the heavens. The causeway has been heavily built up to host ships, and the islands natural shaped of a severely squashed tear helps provide a protective port on either side of this causeway. The city is a large trading port, and large farms spread out on the mainland to one side (north), boardering the coast, while a think anceient forest spreads far south, where jagged snow capped mountains can be seen in the distance. In the middle, a heavily traveled road leads west to the lakes of the golden city. 
Virilion gets its name due to its uncanny location and weather providing an almost constant blanket of mist, heaviest around the back of the island facing the sea and the church. Often the church bell and tips of the church spires are covered in cloud. It rains alot here, not uncommon to see moss growing on newly built buildings. 

The back of the island holds the cemetery?

To the north, the farmlands slowly begin to turn dryer and dryer, the harvest showing different fruits and less luscious colours. The desert begins after around a month on the road north. A week further and you reach the Olin, the city of the sun - (set in an oasis, desert city, home to many strange customs) - beyond this even still, you reach the pillars of the end of the earth - (monolithic structures which reach kilometres into the sky, every inch covered in strange carvings).

To the east, past Virilion, lies an archipelago of islands, before a narrow stretch of open seas which runs into the eastern land of the Madji. Little is known about them, aside from a prosperious trade of spices, fruit, and silk, for the wool and leather of the West.

South - mountains - stretching for an absurb amount inland, and hundreds of kilometers thick....
### Society
Heavily religious society - two main factions
- X : currently in control of the main political power, however the state has been in heavy declination for the past century - people are agitated and loosing faith in the old religion - begin to blame the current god
    
- Y : heretical and somewhat brutal in their beliefs, these are the rising politcal power in the city, seizing hold of this discontent to push their own religion which holds a heavy emphasis on purification through fire/destruction (destroy it to rebuild it) 

### Parallel Dimension
Slip in between worlds, fragmented dimension - defying reality - kalidascopic geometry folds in on itself - physics is warped

Perpetual eclipsed sky, but the moon is cracked and the sun slips through the crack

I want this place to be unstructured and changing - every 10 minutes or so get a player to roll a dice and it triggers a random event -  I want different world slipping through these incomprehendable folds in space - as if the sky ripples and bunches, then burst open - and some cyberpunk buildings or sci fi scenes come through.
