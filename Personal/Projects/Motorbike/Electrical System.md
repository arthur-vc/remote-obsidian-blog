# Control
### Lights ON/OFF
**Controls : 
- Headlight ON/OFF
- Tail Light (Running Light)
- Number plate light
- Speedometer lights
### Indicators
**Controls :**
- Front Left Indicator
- Front Right Indicator
- Back Left Indicator
- Back Right Indicator
PWR delivery will go through flasher BEFORE splitting into 4
### Horn
**Controls :**
- Horn
### High Beams
**Controls :**
- Headlight LOW/HIGH
### Front/Rear Brakes
**Controls :**
- Back Brake Lights
PWR from battery splits into two, goes to front and back brakes, then converges back before brake light so they work independently
